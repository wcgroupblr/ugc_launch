Connect your Google Sheet

Once connected, every change you make here saves to the sheet, and your team sees the same data. Setup takes about 2 minutes and only has to be done once.

  1. Open the Google Sheet, then go to Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Delete the sample code, paste in everything from Code.gs (it's next to this HTML file), and click Save.
  3. Click Deploy → New deployment. Choose type Web app, set Execute as to Me and Who has access to Anyone, then click Deploy and allow access.
  4. Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec) and paste it below.

If the sheet is empty, the researched creators are uploaded to it automatically. Anyone who has this URL can read and edit the sheet's data, so share it only with your team.
